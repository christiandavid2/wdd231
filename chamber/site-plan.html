Website Project Plan

Itlegzi Fashion Boutique

Site Name

Itlegzi Fashion Boutique – This name represents my boutique brand that specializes in modern, elegant, and trendy clothing. The site reflects the brand identity and aims to provide both awareness and easy access to collections.

Site Purpose

The website will showcase the boutique’s latest fashion collections, including clothes and accessories. It will provide style inspiration, product information, and an interactive contact form for customer inquiries or orders. The site will also include a dynamic product listing (from a JSON file) where visitors can browse collections.

Scenarios

  • What trendy outfits are available this season at Itlegzi Fashion Boutique?
  • How can I contact the boutique to place an order or request styling advice?

Color Scheme

Primary: Navy Blue (#2C3E50) – used for headers, navigation, and footer.

Secondary: Warm Orange (#E67E22) – used for buttons, highlights, and accents.

Background: White (#ffffff) – clean background for readability.

Typography

Heading Font: Poppins – stylish and modern, used for titles, headings, and navigation.

Body Font: Roboto – simple and highly readable, used for product descriptions and general content.

Wireframe

Mobile Layout (320px):

Top nav with hamburger menu → Hero banner → Featured collection → Product grid → Contact button → Footer.

Desktop Layout:

Header with logo + nav → Hero section (large banner image) → Featured collections in grid layout → Product list (fetched dynamically) → Contact form preview → Footer.
